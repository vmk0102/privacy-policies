Wali Timer Privacy Policy

Wali Timer is a Scrabble timer application developed by DABBS Solutions (Pvt.) Ltd.

This Privacy Policy explains how Wali Timer handles information when you use the application.

1. Information We Collect

Wali Timer does not require users to create an account and does not directly collect or maintain personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, password, or payment card information on our servers.

The application may store certain settings and preferences locally on your device so that the application can function properly. These locally stored settings are not transmitted to DABBS Solutions.

2. Advertising

Wali Timer uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google's Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and process certain information, which may include:

  • IP address
  • Advertising or device identifiers
  • Information about interactions with advertisements and the application
  • Diagnostic and performance information
  • Information used for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention

This information may be collected and processed by Google and its advertising partners. DABBS Solutions does not independently maintain this information as a personal user database.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, please visit:

Google Privacy Policy

3. In-App Purchases

Wali Timer offers optional support purchases through Google Play Billing.

All purchases are processed by Google Play. Wali Timer and DABBS Solutions do not collect or store users' credit card numbers, debit card numbers, bank account information, or other payment credentials.

We may receive information from Google Play necessary to determine whether a purchase was successfully completed.

4. How We Use Information

Information processed by third-party services may be used for purposes including:

  • Providing advertisements
  • Measuring advertising performance
  • Preventing fraud and abuse
  • Improving service reliability and performance
  • Processing in-app purchases

DABBS Solutions does not sell users' personal information.

5. Data Security

Data transmitted by third-party services used by Wali Timer is handled according to those providers' security practices.

We do not operate a Wali Timer user account database or maintain a database containing users' payment card information.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Wali Timer does not maintain user accounts or a personal user database.

Application preferences stored locally on your device can generally be removed by uninstalling the application or clearing the application's data through your device settings.

Information collected or retained by third-party services such as Google is subject to those services' respective privacy policies and retention practices.

7. Children's Privacy

Wali Timer is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

8. Third-Party Services

Wali Timer uses third-party services, including:

  • Google AdMob — used to provide advertisements.
  • Google Play Billing — used to process optional in-app support purchases.

These services may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies and terms.

9. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights regarding personal data processed by third-party services.

For information about Google's handling of data and available privacy controls, please refer to Google's privacy documentation and your Android device's privacy and advertising settings.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Wali Timer, the services we use, or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will be published at this URL with a revised effective date.
